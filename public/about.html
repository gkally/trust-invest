FAQ

Questions, answered

The things investors ask us most.

How does the 70% return in 60 days work?
When you invest in any of our three plans, your capital is locked for 60 days. At the end of the cycle, you receive your original capital plus a 70% profit — all in one payout.
What's the minimum investment?
The minimum deposit is $10. There's no maximum, though large deposits may be reviewed by our team for security.
How do I deposit funds?
Deposits are made in Bitcoin (BTC). From your dashboard, click "Top Up", enter your USD amount, and we'll show you a live BTC address with a QR code. Once you've sent the funds, submit the transaction hash and our admin will confirm and credit your account.
How do I withdraw my earnings?
When your 60-day cycle ends, your balance is credited to your account. From the dashboard, click "Withdraw" and follow the steps — our admin will process your payout via Bitcoin.
Is my money safe?
We use insured cold wallets, 256-bit encryption, and 24/7 monitoring. Every withdrawal is manually reviewed by our admin team. Your funds are never pooled with other users' balances.
How can I contact the admin?
WhatsApp is our primary support channel. Message us directly at +44 7448 774304 for any question, deposit confirmation, or withdrawal request.
