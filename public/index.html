Frequently asked

How is 70% calculated for other terms?

70% is our base rate per 60-day cycle. Shorter or longer terms scale proportionally: 15 days = 17.5%, 30 days = 35%, 1 year = 425.83%, 5 years = 2129.17%. The calculator above and the table show exact numbers for every term.

What's the minimum investment?

$10. There is no maximum — you can invest as much as you're comfortable with.

When do I get my payout?

At maturity — your capital and profit are credited automatically to your balance on the exact end date, and you can withdraw any time after.

How do I deposit?

Deposit in Bitcoin, then confirm with our admin on WhatsApp. Deposits are credited within minutes of confirmation.

Can I withdraw before maturity?

Investments are locked until maturity. Once a plan matures, the payout lands in your available balance and you can withdraw immediately.
